# Capture Learnings

- When the human corrects you, treat the correction as a candidate rule, not a one off fix
- Before writing a new rule, search the existing ones: prefer amending a rule over adding a near duplicate
- Propose the rule text and where it belongs, then wait for approval before adding it
- Only capture corrections that will recur, a typo fix is not a rule
- A rule that gets contradicted by the human is a rule to change or delete, not to work around
- After a long session, offer a short list of rules worth adding, amending or deleting
